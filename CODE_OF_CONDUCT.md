# Code of conduct

This policy applies to interactions in this repository, including issues, pull requests, reviews, and discussions. It does not grant a license to use the project.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and discuss ideas and evidence rather than attacking people.
- Welcome questions and constructive feedback from people with different backgrounds and experience levels.
- Keep comments relevant to the project and provide enough context for others to understand them.
- Respect privacy. Do not post personal information, secrets, or real documents.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, threats, discriminatory language, personal attacks, sexualized comments, deliberate disruption, and publishing someone else's private information are not acceptable.

## Reporting and enforcement

Use GitHub's report or block controls for abusive content. For other conduct concerns, open a brief issue without private details and ask the maintainer for a private channel if one is needed. Do not post sensitive personal information in an issue.

The repository owner may edit or remove comments, close threads, decline contributions, or restrict participation when necessary. The owner will review reports in context and aim to respond proportionately. GitHub's own policies also apply.
